§1

The sun is setting on another day at sea.
This is the last boat: old, damaged, and crowded with refugees and everything they own. The sail was destroyed days ago, and everyone rows in turns. You are tired. There is little fresh water left, except what the rains give. There is no land in sight, and no way back.
By your bench your three friends rest from their turns at the oars: <A>, <B>, and <C>. Your fourth friend, <D>, sleeps under a tarp, worn out. You decide not to wake <D>.
Your own turn at the oars is over. Behind you, the next rowers settle in and the strokes continue.

<A>'s turn at the oars ends at the same time. <A> drops down beside you and takes a swallow of rainwater from the shared barrel. <A> is not tired, or does not show it.
"The gods did not carry us this far to drown us," says <A>. "We rose against the Dragon Emperor knowing what it would cost, and the gods watched us do it. They will not waste us on the sea. There is a land ahead. I feel it. And when we reach it, it will be ours. We will be the founders of something great. They will tell stories about us."
<A> looks out over the water, and does not blink.
What do you say?

Last, <C>'s turn at the oars ends. <C> sits down with you, wraps a blanket around both of you, and looks up at the sky where the first stars come out between the clouds.
"I have been counting the days," says <C>. "And the ships. Three left home together. The storm took the Wave Dancer. The Emperor's warship took the Dawn Chaser - but her people were pulled onto this boat, so the sea did not get them all."
"And I have been watching the stars. If the maps I remember are right, there should be a coast somewhere south of us. If they are wrong, there is nothing. But the birds went south yesterday, and the water is greener now. Birds do not fly over nothing. Green water is shallow water."
<C> pauses. "I think we are close. I cannot promise it."

The boat grinds over the rocks. The hull tears open with a sound like a living thing dying. People and belongings spill into the black water.
