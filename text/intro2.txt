The sun is setting. This is the last boat. It is old and damaged, and crowded with refugees and their belongings. The sail was destroyed days ago. Everyone rows in turns. Everyone is tired. There is little fresh water left, except what the rains give. There is no land in sight, and no way back.
Near you, your fourth friend D sleeps under a tarp. You decide not to wake her.
Your turn at the oars is over. Behind you, the next rowers settle in and the strokes continue.

"The gods did not carry us this far to drown us," says A. "We rose against the Dragon Emperor knowing what it would cost, and the gods watched us do it. They will not waste us on the sea. There is a land ahead. And when we reach it, it will be ours. We will be the founders of something great. They will tell stories about us."
