The sun is setting on another day at sea. This is the last boat. The voyage took the others. The boat is old and damaged, and crowded with refugees and everything they own. The sail was destroyed days ago, and everyone rows in turns. You are tired. There is little fresh water left, except what the rains give. There is no land in sight, and no way back.
Your turn at the oars is over. Behind you, the next rowers settle in and the strokes continue. You sit down among the others.
